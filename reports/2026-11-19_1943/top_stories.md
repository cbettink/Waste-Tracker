## Top things to know (Tue night Nov 17 – Thu Nov 19)

- **CLH – explosion at Houston-area hazardous waste facility.** An explosion and fire hit the Clean Harbors facility near La Porte/Deer Park, TX on Tuesday night, injuring one worker; the story ran in 37 outlets. The Harris County Fire Marshal says hydrocarbon vapors in a treatment vessel were involved, and that the site had two minor fires the week before. [KHOU / MSN](https://www.msn.com/en-us/money/general/clean-harbors-explosion-hcfmo-says-the-hazardous-waste-facility-had-2-minor-fires-in-past-week/ar-AA2hGUjN) · [Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/news/weather-news/articles/clean-harbors-explosion-hcfmo-says-123231645.html)
- **WCN – Chiquita Canyon aftermath continues.** Hotel stays for affected Val Verde residents were extended, and the company brought in hundreds of extra staff ahead of an incoming storm. A state assemblymember is pushing for stronger oversight, and KCRW reports on the risk of "trash juice" (leachate) flows. Still no new financial disclosure. [Hometown Station](https://www.hometownstation.com/santa-clarita-news/community-news/hotel-rooms-extended-for-residents-impacted-by-chiquita-canyon-landfill-geyser-incident-619468)
- **WM – PureCycle supply deal sized.** WM will supply PureCycle with nearly 25M lbs of polypropylene a year under the multi-year agreement. [Recycling Today](https://www.recyclingtoday.com/purecycle-technologies-and-wm-strike-polypropylene-feedstock-agreement) · [PlasticsToday](https://www.plasticstoday.com/resin-pricing/purecycle-wm-forge-major-plastic-recycling-deal)
- **CWST – board change.** Casella announced a board leadership transition, naming Michael Battles (Clean Harbors co-CEO) Lead Independent Director. [Business Insider](https://markets.businessinsider.com/news/stocks/casella-waste-systems-inc-announces-board-leadership-transition-1036785650)
- **Philadelphia disposal contract.** A City Council committee blocked a new contract with Reworld's Chester incinerator and passed legislation allowing city trash to go only to landfills, a potential volume shift for regional landfill owners. [PhillyVoice](https://www.phillyvoice.com/philly-city-council-trash-chester-incinerator/)
- **Waste Pro loses Cape Coral, FL.** Despite Waste Pro's lawsuit, Cape Coral picked FCC Environmental for its trash contract (about $400M), starting next October. [Gulf Coast News](https://www.gulfcoastnewsnow.com/article/cape-coral-fcc-environmental-trash-hauler-waste-pro/74470346)
- **Landfill capacity and permits.** Lower Saucon Township won another court round against the Bethlehem Landfill expansion (PA), and Linn County, IA plans to ship about 100,000 tons a year to landfills outside the county. [Hoodline](https://hoodline.com/2026/11/lower-saucon-beats-bethlehem-landfill-in-court-expansion-odds-shrink/) · [KGAN / MSN](https://www.msn.com/en-us/news/other/linn-county-plans-to-send-some-trash-to-landfills-outside-the-county/ar-AA2hNOG7)
- **Organics M&A.** Agri-Cycle, backed by Closed Loop Partners' private equity group, expanded into new regions through several acquisitions. [Waste Advantage](https://wasteadvantagemag.com/agri-cycle-expands-into-new-regions-through-multiple-acquisitions/)
- **Pricing.** Another batch of municipal rate hikes: Bloomington, IN (about 50% proposed), Norman, OK, New Braunfels, TX, and Durango, CO (approved), plus FCC Environmental raising rates in Buncombe County, NC. See the Volumes & pricing section.
- **Recycled-content policy.** Waste Dive reviews three years of Washington's PCR law, which is spurring recycled plastic use. Separately, a fire at a railroad-tie recycling facility in Attalla, AL forced evacuations. [Waste Dive](https://www.wastedive.com/news/washington-ecology-recycled-content-policy-penalties/875069/)
