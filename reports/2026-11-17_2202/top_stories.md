## Top things to know (Fri Nov 13 – Tue Nov 17)

- **WCN – methane eruption at Chiquita Canyon.** On Monday a drilling operation hit a pressurized methane pocket, sending a gas-and-debris plume about 500 feet up; six workers were hospitalized with minor injuries. WCN closed down about 8% Tuesday. [Seeking Alpha / MSN](https://www.msn.com/en-us/money/general/waste-connections-plunges-after-methane-gas-eruption-at-los-angeles-area-landfill/ar-AA2hDQiM)
- **WCN – regulatory and political escalation.** LA County Public Health directed the landfill to offer nearby residents immediate relocation; the company is providing hotel rooms and a relief center. An assemblywoman and county supervisors are calling for a state of emergency. More than 60 stories are in the WCN section. [LA Daily News](https://www.dailynews.com/2026/11/16/gas-release-at-chiquita-canyon-landfill-triggers-explosion-in-castaic/)
- **WM – $200M RNG plant opens in Ontario.** The renewable natural gas facility at WM's Twin Creeks landfill (Warwick Township) is now open. [Sarnia Observer](https://www.theobserver.ca/news/renewable-natural-gas-plant-opens-at-twin-creeks-landfill/)
- **WM – multi-year recycled-plastics offtake.** PureCycle signed a rare 3-year bale supply contract with WM. [Resource Recycling](https://resource-recycling.com/plastics/2026/11/16/purecycle-wm-sign-rare-3-year-bales-contract/)
- **RSG – Street and credit.** JPMorgan upgraded RSG to Overweight with a $295 target (reported as Nov 11), and Fitch affirmed its ratings with a stable outlook. [Ad Hoc News](https://www.ad-hoc-news.de/boerse/news/corporate-news/republic-services-stock-jpmorgan-upgrades-rating-to-overweight/70521762) · [Fitch item](https://tradersunion.com/news/companies/show/4239985-fitch-affirms-republic-services-ratings/)
- **GFL – share count and status.** HPS converted all of its Series A preferred into about 6.22M subordinate voting shares (announced Nov 12). A Kalkine Media headline says GFL "decided to stay a public company" after the Nov. 9 bid deadline; no trade outlet in this digest confirms that yet, so check it. [Ad Hoc News](https://www.ad-hoc-news.de/boerse/news/corporate-news/gfl-environmental-stock-6-22-million-shares-issued-after-conversion/70528822)
- **Rumpke – $60M MRF upgrade.** Rumpke plans a $60M rebuild of its Cincinnati-area (St. Bernard) recycling plant, adding optical sorting. Waste Dive also reports that Rumpke opened an Illinois landfill and that a judge dismissed part of the Seneca Meadows lawsuit. [Resource Recycling](https://resource-recycling.com/recycling/2026/11/17/rumpke-to-invest-60m-in-cincinnati-mrf-upgrade/) · [Waste Dive](https://www.wastedive.com/news/landfill-waste-connections-seneca-meadows-emissions-new-york/874593/)
- **Waste Pro – contract fight.** Waste Pro sued Cape Coral, FL over the bid process for a $400M trash contract that the city is set to award to FCC Environmental. [Gulf Coast News](https://www.gulfcoastnewsnow.com/article/cape-coral-trash-contract-lawsuit-waste-pro/74457010)
- **Ontario disposal policy.** Bill 140 would remove the municipal-support requirement for certain large landfills and could open the way to more incineration, which matters for Ontario capacity (WM, GFL, WCN). [CBC / MSN](https://www.msn.com/en-ca/news/other/ontarios-bill-140-raises-concerns-over-landfill-approvals-groundwater-protection-in-caledon-ont/ar-AA2hpW5H)
- **Pricing and tuck-ins.** Municipal fee hikes keep coming: Bloomington, IN is weighing about 50%, and New Braunfels, TX up to 59% by 2030. Private tuck-ins: Ecowaste bought A-1 McDuffie Sanitation (MS), Interstate Waste Services bought Hometown Waste & Recycling, and Generate Capital sold biogas assets to Vanguard Renewables and Pinta Energy. [Waste Today](https://www.wastetodaymagazine.com/ecowaste-expands-in-alabama-mississippi-a1-mcduffie-sanitation-acquisition) · [Waste Advantage](https://wasteadvantagemag.com/interstate-waste-services-acquires-hometown-waste-recycling/)
