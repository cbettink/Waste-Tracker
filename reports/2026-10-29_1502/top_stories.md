## Top things to know

- **WCN – California sues over Chiquita Canyon.** AG Bonta filed suit against Waste Connections over the closed Chiquita Canyon landfill, alleging failures to manage hazardous material and leachate; LA Times reports residents' health complaints are cited. [Waste Dive](https://www.wastedive.com/news/landfill-waste-connections-chiquita-bonta-gfl-wisconsin/873188/)
- **GFL – take-private process enters final stretch.** GFL set a Nov. 9 deadline for take-private offers, with a special committee of independent directors reviewing any deal (also reported by Bloomberg). [Waste Dive](https://www.wastedive.com/news/gfl-sets-nov-9-deadline-for-take-private-offers-touts-strong-q3/873194/)
- **GFL – guidance raised for the third time in 2026.** Q3 driven by pricing gains, stronger organic growth and the SECURE Waste Infrastructure addition. [Waste360](https://www.waste360.com/industry-insights/gfl-lifts-2026-outlook-as-take-private-deadline-nears)
- **CLH – record Q3, guidance raised.** Revenue up 21% year over year per the company release. [Globe and Mail](https://www.theglobeandmail.com/investing/markets/stocks/CLH/pressreleases/8528427/clean-harbors-delivers-record-q3-results-raises-guidance/)
- **RSG – labor.** Republic Services workers in Salinas, CA voted to join Teamsters Local 350; separately, Teamsters representing Orange County waste and public works employees plan to resume their strike. [KFI / MSN](https://www.msn.com/en-us/money/human-resources/teamsters-to-resume-orange-county-strike-as-talks-continue/ar-AA2gimWy)
- **Landfill siting – Bunnell, FL rejects "Project Butterfly."** City commission unanimously rejected the proposed landfill after an ~11-hour hearing; covered by 8 outlets. [Yahoo News](https://www.yahoo.com/news/us/articles/why-did-bunnell-reverse-landfill-163935629.html)
- **Landfill odor litigation in Central Florida.** Lake County is moving ahead with a lawsuit over the Mount Dora-area landfill, and Sumter County may expand its suit against an odor-plagued landfill. See the Landfills section.
- **Ontario disposal capacity.** Ontario's environment minister says the province should burn more garbage and build more landfills, a potential capacity tailwind for GFL and WCN's Canadian operations. See the Landfills section.
- **Battery recycling M&A.** Clarios plans to acquire Ecobat, including three US recycling facilities that process more than 40 million batteries a year. [aftermarketNews](https://www.aftermarketnews.com/clarios-announces-plans-to-acquire-ecobat/)
- **Hauler software funding.** TrashLab, an AI operating system for waste haulers, raised $12M. [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/trashlab-raises-12m-scale-ai-130000556.html)
